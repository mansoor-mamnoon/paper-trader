# Audit of the investment desk, November 2026

On 10 November 2026 the desk queued a buy of `RAMZ` with a stated conviction of 0.90 and
wrote that it was "not a speculative or leveraged play". `RAMZ` is the T-REX 2X Inverse
DRAM Daily Target ETF: it seeks −200% of another fund's daily return. This document maps
each observed failure to the code that caused it. Line numbers refer to commit `f532ef4`,
the last commit before the repair.

## Root-cause map

| # | Failure | Where it started | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | A leveraged inverse ETF was screened, researched and bought as a stock. | `data::parse_directory` (`src/data.rs:44`) stored each symbol's registered name and ETF flag; `account::screen` (`src/account.rs:853`) never read them. | The screen iterated `observation.quotes` and applied price, liquidity and event gates only. Nothing downstream knew what a symbol was: `desk::dossier` (`src/desk.rs:181`) printed the ticker and numbers, so the judge saw "RAMZ" and guessed. The account's first trade, `AAOZ`, was a 2x inverse fund for the same reason. |
| B | News about Shaver Shop Group (ASX: SSG) was used as evidence for ProShares UltraShort Semiconductors (NYSE Arca: SSG). | `intel::fetch_news` (`src/intel.rs:80`) and `intel::parse_rss` (`src/intel.rs:68`). | The feed is requested by ticker text (`?s=SSG`) and every returned title was attached to the symbol. `parse_rss` kept the title and date and dropped the link, id and summary, so there was nothing to match an issuer against or to audit later. |
| C | Triage said "positive news" and the judge said "tangible catalyst" for a fund with no headlines. | `desk::TRIAGE` (`src/desk.rs:270`), `desk::JUDGE_RULES` (`src/desk.rs:292`), `desk::dossier` (`src/desk.rs:201`). | The triage prompt asks for a catalyst score for every stock and a dossier with no news simply omits the news lines, so "no evidence" was invisible. No code checked a model's claim against the evidence it was given. |
| D | Bull and bear repeated the dossier ("momentum is strong" / "volatility is high"). | `desk::BULL`, `desk::BEAR` (`src/desk.rs:285-290`) and `desk::argue` (`src/desk.rs:565`). | Both sides got the same dossier and a one-line instruction to argue for or against. The bear never saw the bull's argument, and neither was asked for a mechanism, an assumption or a falsifier. |
| E | A conviction of 0.90 was recorded and shown as if it meant something. | `desk::schema_judge` (`src/desk.rs:251`), `account::cycle_with` (`src/account.rs:699`), `web/app.js:472`. | The number is free model output. It was written into the order reason ("Analyst conviction 0.90"), bucketed as "calibration" in `scorecard::summary` (`src/scorecard.rs:135`) from the first sample, and fed back to the judge. |
| F | Missing data was read as a fact: "no earnings in 14 days" as a risk, a Form 4 as insider buying, raw filing headers as fundamentals. | `desk::dossier` (`src/desk.rs:199`), `edgar::describe` (`src/edgar.rs:281`), `edgar::filing_text` (`src/edgar.rs:159`), `desk::TRIAGE`. | The dossier printed "Earnings: none in the next 14 days" as a finding. `describe` labelled every Form 4 "insider transaction" and the triage prompt rewards "filings that suggest insiders ... are buying", with no transaction code read. `filing_text` returned the first 2,400 characters of the stripped document, which for inline XBRL is the hidden header. |
| G | The position doctor said "hold" on `AAOZ` in the run that sold it. | `daily::run` block 4 (`src/daily.rs:489`) and `desk::held_views` (`src/desk.rs:895`). | Not a ledger error: see the reconciliation below. The doctor is briefed from the state loaded before the cycle and is not told that an exit is already queued, so it reviews positions the rules have already decided to sell. |

Three further defects were found while tracing the flows:

| # | Defect | Where |
|---|---|---|
| H | Forward returns in the scorecard are wrong across a split. | `scorecard::update` (`src/scorecard.rs:98`) divides a later, split-adjusted close by the unadjusted price recorded on the pick date. |
| I | A symbol whose last bar is up to five days old can be bought. | `account::screen` (`src/account.rs:859`) and `account::cycle_with` (`src/account.rs:374`) only skip bars older than five days, and 20-day momentum assumes 21 consecutive sessions. |
| J | The lab can promote a strategy on two fills. | `lab::decide` (`src/lab.rs:222`): 14 days, `MIN_FILLS = 2`, a one-point edge, no measure of uncertainty. |

## The AAOZ reconciliation

The ledger on the `account` branch shows one consistent sequence:

| Run (UTC) | Latest bar | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| 10 Nov 06:36 | 9 Nov, close $29.88 | The buy queued on 7 Nov filled at the 9 Nov open ($32.345). The close was below the $31.05 stop, so `SELL_SIGNAL` was queued. |
| 10 Nov 07:48 | 9 Nov, close $29.88 | The doctor was asked about `AAOZ`, which already had a queued sell, and answered "hold". Recorded as `DOCTOR_HOLD`; it changed nothing. |
| 10 Nov 22:33 | 10 Nov, close $34.40 | The doctor, briefed from the pre-cycle state, saw a 6.35% gain and answered "hold". The cycle then filled the queued sell at the 10 Nov open ($33.343 after costs, +$5.76). |

The two transcripts describe two different sessions, and the stop, the queued exit and the
fill were all correct. The defect is that the doctor was consulted on a position with a
mandatory exit pending and its "hold" was published beside a position that the same run
sold. The doctor could not have cancelled the sell (`cycle_with` fills queued orders
before it reads doctor calls), but nothing tested that.

## OMH

`OMH` (Ohmyhome) completed a 1-for-50 reverse split effective 31 August 2026. The price
provider's bars are split-adjusted on both sides of that date (28 Aug close $3.50, 31 Aug
open $3.53) and its volumes are adjusted the same way, so traded value is unchanged. The
+432% move on 4 November came with volume of 93 million shares against about 20,000 the
day before and is not a split artefact. The displayed returns were computed consistently.
What was missing is a check that would have caught the opposite case, and defect H above.
