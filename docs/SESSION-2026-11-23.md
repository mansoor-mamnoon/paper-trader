## Repairs and verification boundaries

The analyst now builds the current case from current sources. The skeptic also receives the dated original entry headline, and the manager receives that headline alongside the holding's material current headlines. Stock files list observed trading dates. The exit checker retains the full historical dossier.

Scouts now select a dated source headline available by the signal cutoff for their sector driver. Only sectors with a cited driver can enter focus, and the strategist can leave the focus list empty. The skeptic must supply an exact passage for a new purchase to qualify. Rust checks it against the analyst's cited headline or the selected sector driver. A missing or mismatched passage excludes the candidate even with a weakened verdict. An exact match establishes provenance. Judging whether the passage supports the business claim still depends on the model.

The overnight scheduled repeat skipped the already-completed session. GitHub confirmed the Pages artifact upload and then reported it missing during deployment. Publication now retries the saved artifact once after a delay. No additional inference or order processing is involved.
