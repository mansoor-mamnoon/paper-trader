## Repairs and verification boundaries

The analyst now builds the current case from current sources. The skeptic also receives the dated original entry headline, and the manager receives that headline alongside the holding's material current headlines. Stock files list observed trading dates. The exit checker retains the full historical dossier.

Scouts now select a source headline for their sector driver. Only sectors with a cited driver can enter focus. The skeptic must supply an exact passage for a new purchase to qualify. Rust checks it against the analyst's cited headline or the selected sector driver. A missing or mismatched passage excludes the candidate even with a weakened verdict. An exact match establishes provenance. Judging whether the passage supports the business claim still depends on the model.
