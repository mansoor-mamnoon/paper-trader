A rulebook, not a promise

How it makes decisions

Every five minutes, the worker reads public Kraken prices for BTC, ETH, SOL and ADA. It buys only when the 20-hour moving average is above the 50-hour average and the last 24-hour return exceeds 1%, using completed hourly candles only.

A signal queues an order. It can fill only on a later observation with a newer trade timestamp, at least 60 seconds later and no more than an hour later. A weakening trend queues a delayed exit. Stops, targets and a seven-day time limit are standing exit instructions. Each exit starts a 24-hour cooldown.

What this experiment cannot tell you

This is deterministic automation, not an LLM choosing trades. No claim of a profitable edge has been established. Conservative modeled fees and fills still cannot reproduce real liquidity, queue priority, exchange outages or market impact.

The public dashboard is read-only. This Mac must be awake, online and logged in for its background agents to run. After downtime it resumes at current quotes; it never invents missed fills. The free public tunnel has no uptime guarantee and its URL can change when it restarts.
