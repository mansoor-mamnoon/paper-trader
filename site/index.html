          Problems and events

            One trading day

            1. Settle queued orders at the open, apply fees and slippage, and update holdings.
            2. Screen US stocks using news, Reddit mentions, trending lists and trading activity. FinBERT scores headline sentiment.
            3. Write sector notes and select candidates. Classify their headlines, then research each stock with an analyst and skeptic.
            4. Review every holding and choose purchases. Check the evidence for orders and queue them for the next open.

                Models and scheduling

                NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B handles all six research roles. Failed hosted questions go to local Qwen3.5 9B. GitHub Actions runs after the US close, with catch-up triggers and a watchdog for missed sessions. Scheduling delays and provider outages can interrupt a run.

                Project notes

                The README covers setup and operation. Detailed reviews are in the project records. No broker is connected. Free feeds can miss news, and models can make mistakes. Nothing here is investment advice.
